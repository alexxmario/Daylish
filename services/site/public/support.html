Quick answers

A food's calories look wrong

Check where the entry came from first. Packaged foods come from the barcode via Open Food Facts, which is a public database that anyone can contribute to, so a wrong packet occasionally gets in. Whole foods come from USDA FoodData Central and are laboratory-measured. If an entry is an estimate rather than a lookup, Daylish labels it as one. Send us the food name and we will look at it.

The barcode scanner will not read a packet

Daylish needs camera permission — check Settings → Daylish → Camera. If the barcode scans but no food comes back, that product is not in Open Food Facts yet. Use Quick add to enter the numbers from the packet; it takes about fifteen seconds and the entry is yours from then on.

I got a new phone. Where is my diary?

Install Daylish and sign in with the same email address. Your diary is restored automatically onto a phone that is holding nothing for that account. Restore runs once, on a fresh install — it is a backup and restore, not a live sync, so using two phones at the same time is not supported and the second phone will not merge into the first.

My targets changed and I did not change them

That is adaptive targeting doing its job. Daylish compares your 14-day weight trend against what you have logged and adjusts. Every adjustment comes with a sentence explaining why it happened — open Progress to read it. Adaptive targets are a Premium feature; on the free tier your targets stay where onboarding put them.

I bought Premium and the app still shows a paywall

Tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen. This is also the route back after reinstalling or moving to a new phone, and it is the only route for the lifetime purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us with the Apple ID email you purchased under.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they are cancelled in Apple's settings, not in Daylish: open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also reach the subscription management screen from Daylish under You → Account → Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, and it is free on every tier. You → Export writes your whole diary — foods, journal entries, weight, water, fasting, saved meals and recipe activity — to a JSON file you can keep or move anywhere. Holding a diary hostage to a subscription is the one thing that would make the privacy promise a lie.

Does Daylish work without a connection?

Yes, once you have signed in. The diary lives in a database on the phone, so logging, editing, recipes and targets all work offline. The two things that need a connection are looking up a food you have not logged before, and backing your diary up to your account — both catch up on their own when you are back online.

Why do I need an account at all?

So your diary survives a lost phone. The account holds a backup of your diary and nothing else — it is not used for advertising, analytics or anything you did not ask for. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored.

I want to delete my account

It is in the app, and it is immediate — see deleting your account for the steps and for exactly what is removed.

Reporting a problem with a recipe

The recipe library ships inside the app and its nutrition is computed from the ingredients rather than typed in, so a wrong number usually means a wrong ingredient quantity. Send the recipe name and what looks off. Allergen and diet filtering is safety-critical — if you find a recipe filed under the wrong allergen or diet, please tell us and it will be treated as urgent.
