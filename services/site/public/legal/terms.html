2. What Daylish is

Daylish records what you eat, drink and weigh, computes nutrition figures from public reference data, and estimates calorie and macronutrient targets from what you record. It includes a library of recipes whose nutrition is calculated from their ingredients.

Nutrition data comes from USDA FoodData Central and from Open Food Facts, a public database that anyone may contribute to. We compute carefully from those sources but we do not warrant that every figure is correct, and figures for packaged products depend on data we did not create. Allergen and dietary filtering is provided in good faith and is based on recipe ingredient data; if you have a serious allergy, always check the ingredients yourself before eating anything.
